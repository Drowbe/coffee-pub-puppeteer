# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Each entry names its verification. "Simulated" means `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` passes against a stand-in for Foundry and the change has not yet been run in Foundry.


## [14.0.0]

### Fixed
- **Double-clicking a pin opens its group.** The Group Editor opens on Scene Groups with that group's card open and at the top of the list, and holds it there after the window restores its scroll position, which could carry it back to where the list was. The Make a New Pin button on a group that lost its pin carried the text `${esc(group.id)}` instead of the group's id, so it did nothing; it now has the id. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Fabricated and duplicated groups get their own name.** A group fabricated from the Library, or duplicated, whose name is already on the scene is numbered ("Wagon 2", "Wagon 3", and "Wagon copy 2"), compared without regard to case. Its token, if it has one, is named after the group, so two fabrications are told apart when they are targeted; an unlinked token (the default) has its own hit points, and a linked one shares its actor's. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/library.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **A fabricated group keeps how the original behaved.** A Library entry held the parts and the body's actor and nothing else, so a group fabricated from it came back with Tokens board when they move onto it off, and with default movement, facing, events and the rest. The template now carries the group's behaviour (movement, turning, facing and its front, boarding, announcing, solid parts, riders moving freely, size and events), and an event that changes a tile's picture is pointed at the new group's own tile, which also fixes a duplicate whose events pointed at the original's tile. An entry saved before this has none of it and must be saved again. Files: `scripts/template.js`, `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/library.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **A moving group no longer flashes at its destination first.** When a group's documents were written, the glide began drawing on the next frame, so for that frame Foundry's own redraw showed the group already at the end of its move. The group is now drawn where it was the moment the first part is written, and a part that arrives after the glide began is drawn where the glide has it. Files: `scripts/tween.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/pinglide.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Deleting a pin deletes its group.** Deleting a group's pin from Blacksmith's pin menu, or deleting pins in bulk, now deletes the group by the same route as the Delete button: its record, its body token and its parts go, and its riders are let go where they stand. A notice says so, and nothing is asked first. Before, the group was left on its card without a pin. Make a New Pin stays for a group that lost its pin another way. Files: `scripts/prune.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/pindelete.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Fabricating a group could swap two parts.** A stamp tied each new part to the document Foundry returned in the same position, and Foundry does not promise that order, so two tiles could trade places (a wagon came back with its canopy where the horse was and the horse where the canopy was, both turned). Each part is now given its own id before it is made, so the tie cannot be wrong. Files: `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated, with documents returned in the opposite order (`tests/library.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Capture Area picks regions.** Blacksmith's area pick now lists regions, and Capture Area asks for every type the pick knows, so regions inside the box join the group with no change to Puppeteer. Files: none. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.

### Changed
- **Parts shows what is selected.** Under Parts the rows are Add (Capture Area, Add Selected, Remove Selected) and then Select (Tiles, Lights, Regions and so on), each labelled. A part or rider that is selected on the map is marked in the list, a Select button is lit when every part of its kind is selected, and a count says how many of the group's parts are selected. Clicking a part selects it on the map and so marks it. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Add Event is a dialog.** The row of selects and fields under Events is one **Add Event** button that opens a Blacksmith dialog where nothing is pre-chosen: every list starts at Choose one, and the rest appears as you choose (the health level for a health event, what to do, then only the fields that action needs (a sound with a browse button, a tile and a picture, a macro)). A rule that cannot be added is refused with its reason and the dialog opens again with what was typed. The card's menu button is the vertical ellipsis, like the one in the window's title bar. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **A More menu on each group card, and a one-line Resize.** Centre Pivot, Move Pivot, Duplicate, Save to Library, Export and Delete are in a "..." menu in the card's bar (Blacksmith's context menu), instead of four icons in the bar and two buttons under Position. Resize is one line: Smaller and Bigger as icons, the step with its × inside the field, the width in squares with an icon, and Reset as an icon, with thin rules between them. With Group Bounds on, the side that the width measures is drawn heavier and labelled Width. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/overlay.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Resize has Smaller, Bigger, Reset and a width.** "Resize about the pin" is now "Resize". Smaller and Bigger are opposites by one step you set (1.25 to begin with), replacing the fixed 0.5, 0.8, 1.25 and 2 whose opposites did not exist; Reset puts a group back to the size it had, using the total the group now remembers (`scale`); Width shows the group's width in squares and resizes the group to the number you type. From a macro: `resetScale` and `setWidth`. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`, `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/scale.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **The on and off switches sit on a grid.** Behaviour, Boarding and Hit points options are laid out in equal columns, a switch and its label to a cell, instead of flowing along the line. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Icon-only card bar.** Go To Pin, Hide or Show, and Play or Pause are icons in a group card's bar, with tooltips, like the rest of the buttons there. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Group card order, and Library and Export in the bar.** An open group card now shows Token, Riders, Behaviour, Position (formerly Pivot and heading), Events, then Parts. Save to Library and Export are icons in the card's bar, the Library icon being the one on the Library tab, instead of buttons at the bottom of the card. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Replace from Group.** A library entry has one button, Replace from Group, instead of a list and a button. It opens a list of the scene's groups; choosing one replaces the entry's parts, token and picture with it, and Cancel backs out. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: the replacement is simulated (`tests/library-edit.test.mjs`); the dialog is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Click a part or a rider to select it.** In the Group Editor the whole row of a part, or of a rider, selects it on the map (Shift adds to the selection), switching to its layer and panning to it if it is off screen. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/groups.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/select.test.mjs`).
- **The Group Editor uses Blacksmith's own components.** Its tabs (underline tabs with a count), list rows and badges are Blacksmith's (`blacksmith-tool-tabs`, `blacksmith-list`, `blacksmith-badge`) and Blacksmith's tool-window controls (`blacksmith-tool-btn` for every button, `blacksmith-tool-segmented` for the Gliding or Jumping choice and, as `blacksmith-toggle` switches, its on/off options (Turns with its pin, Announce boarding, Riders move freely, Tokens board, Linked), `blacksmith-tool-card` for each group, `blacksmith-tool-section`, `blacksmith-tool-hint`, `blacksmith-tool-empty`, `blacksmith-tool-editable` for names that are text until edited, `blacksmith-tool-dropzone` for the body's drop target, and the themed footer buttons), so it follows the Light, Dark and Glass themes without rules of its own, and the stylesheet is a fraction of its former size. The body now has the 8px of padding the tool shell leaves to each window. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: simulated; the look is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Riders stay aboard through a long move.** Solid parts only asked a tile's mesh about a point as the mesh is drawn, which during and just after a move is where the tile used to be, so a rider carried far from where the picture was drawn could be judged off the group and let go. The question is now carried from the tile's pose to the mesh's first, and a rider that sits exactly where the group carries it is never taken for one moved by hand. Files: `scripts/riders.js`, `scripts/boarding.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/solid.test.mjs`, `tests/riders.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Travelling happens on the drop, and a group that faces its travel turns as it goes.** A travelling group no longer follows the pointer while its pin is dragged: it stays put and travels at its steady pace when the pin is dropped, as a token does. The glide now carries where the follower is putting the group (`to`), so a group that faces its travel eases to its new heading along with its position instead of snapping round at the end, and a gliding group turns toward the pointer while it is dragged. The facing test compares headings round the circle. Files: `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/anchor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/pinglide.test.mjs`, `tests/behaviour.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.

### Added
- **Play and Pause are events.** A group's Pause button (Play when paused) and the Play or Pause Group item on its pin menu now show only when the group has an event for them. New events: the triggers "Play is clicked" and "Pause is clicked", and the actions "Start the animations" and "Stop the animations", beside Play a sound, Run a macro and Show a picture on a tile, so a group decides for itself what playing and pausing do (stop a video, play a sound, both, or a macro). `setPlaying` runs the events and returns `{ playing, ran }`. Files: `scripts/events.js`, `scripts/animations.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/ui.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/playback.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Front of the picture.** A group that faces its travel took the bottom of its picture as its front, so a cart drawn pointing up went backwards. Behaviour now has Front of the picture (Down, Left, Up, Right, and a degrees field to fine tune), shown when Faces its travel is on; `front` in `setBehaviour`. Files: `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/playback.test.mjs`).
- **Solid parts only.** A token boards, and stays aboard, only where the picture is not transparent: the transparent corners of a ship's tile no longer count as the deck. It uses Foundry's own per-pixel test on the tile (`containsCanvasPoint`, alpha 0.25 or the tile's own threshold if higher), is on by default, and has a toggle under Behaviour (`solidOnly` in `setBehaviour`) that turns it off for the whole box. Where the tile is not drawn (another scene is on screen) it falls back to the box. Files: `scripts/riders.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/library.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/solid.test.mjs`), and seen working in Foundry for boarding by the author.
- **Resize a group.** Four buttons under Pivot and heading (0.5×, 0.8×, 1.25×, 2×) grow or shrink a group about its pin: parts, lights, sounds, drawings and regions scale with their distance from the pin, riders stand further out or closer in at their own size, and the token is refitted. Doubling and then halving restores everything, bar whole-pixel sizes of drawings. From a macro: `scale`. Files: `scripts/geometry.js`, `scripts/region-shapes.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/scale.test.mjs`).
- **Damage levels.** Events can wait for the group's token to reach a health level (Healthy, Injured, Bloodied, Critical, Dead, using Blacksmith's own lines between them) and a new action shows a different picture on one of the group's tiles, so a cart can become a wreck when it is Bloodied and be mended when it is Healthy again. A hit that skips a level runs only the level it lands on. Files: `scripts/health.js`, `scripts/events.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/bootstrap.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: simulated (`tests/health.test.mjs`); not yet seen in Foundry with a real system's actors.
- **Group events.** A group can do something when something happens to it: when a token boards or leaves, when the group moves, or when it is hidden or shown, play a sound for everyone or run a macro. Rules are listed and added under Events on the group's card, up to twenty a group, and a failing rule is reported and skipped without stopping the move. A duplicate keeps them. From a macro: `addEvent` and `removeEvent`. Files: `scripts/events.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: the rules and when they fire are simulated (`tests/events.test.mjs`); the form is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **`getState` in the API.** A read-only look at a group: where its pin is and which way it faces, whether it is moving and how fast (squares a second), how far through the move it is, whether it is hidden, and how many parts, riders and tokens it has. For a macro that changes a sound or a light with the group's speed. Files: `scripts/state.js`, `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/state.test.mjs`).
- **Faces its travel.** A Behaviour option that turns a group to face the way its pin was moved, with the pin turning to match; very short moves, and moves where the pin was also turned by hand, are left alone. A group's front is the bottom of its picture, as a token's is. From a macro: `setBehaviour(groupId, { facesTravel: true })`. Files: `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/facing.test.mjs`).
- **More on the pin menu.** Right-clicking a group's pin now offers Hide or Show Group, Switch Group Lights On or Off, Switch Group Sounds On or Off (the last two only when the group has any) and Duplicate Group. Lights and sounds flip together. From a macro: `setEmittersOn`. Files: `scripts/ui.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: the switching is simulated (`tests/hide.test.mjs`); the menu is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Make a New Pin.** A group whose pin was deleted shows a button on its card that puts a new pin where the group last was, facing the way it faced; nothing moves and the group follows the new pin. From a macro: `replacePin`. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/replacepin.test.mjs`).
- **Travelling.** A third way for a group to move: at a steady pace, as a token does, so the time follows the distance. Choose Travelling under Moves by and set the pace in squares a second (6 by default, a token's own pace). A turn takes about a second for a half turn, and the longer of the move and the turn sets the time. Tiles, drawings, notes and riders travel; lights, walls, sounds and regions still update at the start of the move, as before. Files: `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/behaviour.test.mjs`).
- **A thumbnail for each library entry.** Saving or updating an entry draws a small picture of the group from its own parts (the tiles' images where they sit, with other parts as marks) and shows it in the Library. An entry without one shows a placeholder. Files: `scripts/thumbnail.js`, `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/template.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: the plan is simulated (`tests/thumbnail.test.mjs`); the drawing itself is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Duplicate a group.** A copy button on each group's card makes a new group two squares to the right: new parts and pin, the same heading and behaviour, its own body if the original has one, and no riders. A hidden group is copied shown. From a macro: `duplicate`. Files: `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/duplicate.test.mjs`).
- **Group cards.** Each group in the Group Editor is a card that is one line when closed (name, parts, heading, Go To Pin, Hide or Show, delete) and opens as a unit into panels for Parts, Pivot and heading, Body, Riders and Behaviour. The list of parts is always shown when a card is open, and open cards stay open across redraws. A group made in the editor opens. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: simulated; the layout itself is not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Hide and show a group.** Hide hides a group's tiles, drawings, regions and body token and turns its lights and sounds off, and Show brings back what it hid, leaving anything that was already hidden hidden. Each part has its own eye. Walls and notes have no hidden state in Foundry and riders are never hidden. From a macro: `setHidden` and `setMemberHidden`. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/hide.test.mjs`).
- **Edit the library.** Library entries are cards with a name you can change, a description, and Update from group, which replaces an entry's parts with a group on the scene and leaves copies already placed alone. Stamp is now called Fabricate. From a macro: `library.rename`, `library.describe` and `library.updateFromGroup`. Files: `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/library-edit.test.mjs`).
- **Behaviour options.** Each group has a Behaviour section: whether it moves by gliding (with a duration from 100 to 3000 ms) or jumping, whether it turns when its pin turns (off for a platform that slides without turning), whether riders may move about inside it or are put back, and whether boarding and leaving are announced in chat with a sound. Riders glide along with the group. From a macro: `setBehaviour`. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/riders.js`, `scripts/boarding.js`, `scripts/announce.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/behaviour.test.mjs`, `tests/announce.test.mjs`).
- **A Puppeteer menubar button, a toast channel, and sounds.** The menubar has one Puppeteer button, in the middle zone with the other Coffee Pub tools, that opens a bar with Group Editor, New Group, Update All Groups and a Group Bounds switch that lights while the bounds are shown. Every Puppeteer toast is on a `puppeteer` channel that a GM can switch off in Blacksmith's settings. Stamping a template, and a Capture Area that adds members, play a soft sound on the acting GM's screen. Files: `scripts/ui.js`, `scripts/overlay.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`, `scripts/constants.js`, `scripts/announce.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/announce.test.mjs`, `tests/menubar.test.mjs`); the button and bar are not yet seen in Foundry.
- **Move Pivot.** Put a group's pin anywhere, such as the prow of a ship, a wagon's hitch or a door's hinge. Turn on Move Pivot for the group and drag the pin; the pin moves, every part, rider and the body stay where they are, and Move Pivot switches itself off. From a macro: `setMovePivot`. Files: `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/movepivot.test.mjs`).
- **Double-click a pin to open its group.** Double-clicking a group's pin opens the Group Editor on that group with its members showing. GM only. Files: `scripts/ui.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: not yet run in Foundry.
- **Riders.** A group can carry tokens, such as a ship's crew. With Tokens board when they move onto it turned on, a token that moves onto the group, or is created on it, boards it; Board Selected does the same by hand. Riders are carried when the group moves, swing round the pin when it turns, can be walked anywhere inside the group, and dismount where they stand when walked off. A token dismounted on purpose stays off until it has left the group and come back. From a macro: `mount`, `dismount` and `setMountable`. Files: `scripts/riders.js`, `scripts/boarding.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/prune.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: dropping an actor on a group and Board Selected, in Foundry by the author; the rest simulated (`tests/riders.test.mjs`).
- **Library entries remember their actor.** Saving a group that has a body keeps the actor, and every stamp comes with its own body for it, sized to that stamp. The Library lists which actor an entry represents, and a stamp in a world without that actor has no body and says so. Files: `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/template.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/body.test.mjs`).
- **Make a group something that can be attacked.** Drag an actor onto a group's body row in the Group Editor, or choose Use Selected Token, and the group gets a body: an invisible, locked token of that actor, as big as the group, that follows it and is refitted when parts change. The actor's picture never shows, and the token is unlinked unless Linked is ticked. From a macro: `setBody`, `adoptBody`, `clearBody` and `setBodyLinked`. Files: `scripts/body.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/prune.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`, `images/transparent.png`. Verified: the body following a pin drag, in Foundry by the author; the rest simulated (`tests/body.test.mjs`).
- **The grip is always a pin.** A group is moved and turned by its pin. A group saved with a token as its anchor, in an earlier build, is converted when the world loads: it gets a pin where the token was, nothing moves, and the token is left as an ordinary token. Files: `scripts/migrate.js`, `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/groups.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/migrate.test.mjs`).
- **Smooth pin groups.** Dragging a pin, the tiles, drawings and notes of its group follow the pointer, and when a pin moves or turns any other way every player sees the group ease to its new place over about a third of a second, turning along an arc. Files: `scripts/tween.js`, `scripts/follower.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/pinglide.test.mjs`).
- **Turn a group with the mouse wheel.** A group faces where its pin faces. Select the pin and hold Shift while scrolling over the board: the group turns about the pin, 5 degrees a notch. The editor's heading field and rotate buttons turn the same pin. Needs the Blacksmith version with pin rotation. Files: `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/migrate.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/pinrotation.test.mjs`); not yet run in Foundry.
- **Click a member to select it, and see member ids.** The icon beside each member in the Group Editor selects that object on the canvas, switching layer and panning to it; each member shows its id, and clicking it copies the UUID. Renaming a member never changes its id. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/select.test.mjs`).
- **A tool window with tabs.** The Group Editor is a Blacksmith tool window, so it follows the Light, Dark and Glass themes and keeps its place. Its tabs are Library, Scene Groups and Tools. Show group bounds is an icon in the title bar. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `styles/group-editor.css`. Verified: the window opens and its group controls work, in Foundry by the author; the tabs and the Tools tab are not yet checked.
- **Deleting groups.** Deleting a group offers to delete its parts from the scene too. The group's pin and body are always removed and riders are let go. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/store.test.mjs`).
- **Regions in groups.** A region can be a member, so zones such as a campsite or a trap area move and turn with the rest. Every shape in the region is carried: rectangles, ellipses, circles, cones, rings, lines, polygons and emanations. A region with a token or grid-cell shape is refused with a reason. A region reshaped by hand, such as one given a cutout, is recorded again where it stands, and the bounds overlay draws each shape as its own outline. Files: `scripts/region-shapes.js`, `scripts/geometry.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/overlay.js`. Verified: regions as members, in Foundry by the author; the cutout outlines are simulated (`tests/region.test.mjs`).
- **Tiles are placed correctly in v14.** A v14 tile's `x, y` is its texture anchor point, the centre by default, and it turns about that point. Puppeteer had treated it as the top-left corner, so a tile was half its size out whenever a group turned or a template was stamped. Group records saved before this are repaired from where the tiles are when the world loads, and exported templates from before are refused with a clear message (template format version 2). Files: `scripts/geometry.js`, `scripts/migrate.js`, `scripts/template.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author, and simulated (`tests/geometry.test.mjs`, `tests/migrate.test.mjs`).
- **Failures are no longer silent.** An error while moving or turning a group, or while centring its pivot, is shown to the GM and handed back to whoever asked. Centre Pivot checks that the pin really moved and puts the group back if it did not. A member that another module stops from moving raises one notification. Files: `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/groups.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/follower.test.mjs`).
- **Library and stamping.** Save a group to a library kept in a world setting, then stamp it onto any scene, as many times as wanted. Each stamp creates new tiles, lights, sounds, walls, a new pin and a new group, so two stamps are two independent copies. Templates can be imported from and exported to JSON files. Files: `scripts/library.js`, `scripts/template.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/library.test.mjs`, `tests/template.test.mjs`); not yet run in Foundry.
- **Group bounds overlay.** An optional overlay draws the box that holds each group, turned with the group, its pivot and the way it faces. Toggled from the toolbar, the Group Editor or the pin menu, and remembered per user. Files: `scripts/overlay.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Member names.** Each member can be given a name in the Group Editor; the name survives hand edits and travels in templates. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/group-editor.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Diagnosis.** `api.diagnose(groupId)` reports where a group's pin, record and members are against where they should be. Files: `scripts/groups.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Contributor checks.** `node tools/check-scripts.mjs` (scripts parse as ES modules and every import resolves), `node tools/check-blacksmith-only.mjs` (fails on core calls Blacksmith replaces, such as `Hooks.on`, `game.socket` and `ui.notifications`), and `node --test tests/*.test.mjs`. Verified: `check-blacksmith-only` failed on a native `Hooks.once('init')` during development, and `check-scripts` caught a duplicate import that broke the Group Editor.
- **Group templates.** A group can be exported as a portable template (offsets and a snapshot of every member), saved as JSON from the Group Editor, and turned into creation data for any pivot and heading. Files: `scripts/template.js`. Verified: simulated (`tests/template.test.mjs`).
- **Centre Pivot.** Moves a group's pin to the centre of what it holds without moving any member. In the Group Editor and the pin menu. Files: `scripts/groups.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Manual edits are kept.** Moving, turning or resizing a member by hand updates where it sits in the group, instead of being undone on the next move. Capturing waits for the group to settle first. Files: `scripts/follower.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Capture Area.** Drag a box and every tile, drawing, light, sound, wall and note whose centre is inside joins the group, across all layers, using Blacksmith's area pick. - **Group Editor, toolbar and pin menu.** A Group Editor window lists the groups on the scene with their members, heading, rename, add and remove selected, select members, go to pin and delete. The Blacksmith toolbar has Puppeteer Groups and New Group tools, and right-clicking a group's pin offers Add Selected, Remove Selected, Select Members, Rotate 90 either way and Open Group Editor. Deleting a member's document removes it from its groups. Files: `scripts/group-editor.js`, `scripts/ui.js`, `scripts/prune.js`. Verified: the Group Editor, in Foundry by the author; the toolbar tools and pin menu are not yet checked.
- **Pin-anchored groups.** A group is a record in a scene flag, anchored on a Blacksmith pin. Dragging the pin, or changing the group's heading, moves and rotates every member (Tile, Drawing, AmbientLight, AmbientSound, Wall, Note) rigidly. The active GM applies it from `updateScene`, one queued write per group. Files: `scripts/groups.js`, `scripts/follower.js`, `scripts/store.js`, `scripts/anchor.js`, `scripts/frame.js`, `scripts/geometry.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Bootstrap.** Puppeteer waits for Blacksmith, refuses to start if its ready stage failed or any API member it uses is missing, registers its console label and registers itself with Blacksmith. Files: `scripts/bootstrap.js`, `scripts/blacksmith.js`, `scripts/puppeteer.js`. Verified: in Foundry by the author.
- **Module scaffold.** Folder layout, release and wiki workflows, and the documentation tools. Files: `module.json`, `.github/workflows/`, `tools/`. Verified: the module loads in Foundry with no errors.

### Removed
- **Groups anchored on a token.** The earlier mode in which a token was a group's grip is gone, with Attach to Token, On Selected Token, Detach from Token, the token follower, the token glide and the question asked when such a token was deleted. See "The grip is always a pin" above for how existing groups are converted. A player can no longer drive a cart by moving a token; a player given edit access to a group's pin can move it, without walls or a movement ruler.
