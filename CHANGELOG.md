# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).


## [14.0.0]

### Added
- **Behaviour options.** Each group has a Behaviour section. Choose whether it moves by gliding (with your own duration) or jumping, whether it turns when its pin turns (off for a platform that should slide without turning), whether riders may move about inside it or are put back, and whether boarding and leaving are announced in chat with a sound. Riders now glide along with the group. From a macro: `setBehaviour`.
- **Menubar buttons, a toast channel, and sounds.** The menubar has a button that shows or hides the group bounds, lighting up while they are shown, and a button that opens the Group Editor. Every Puppeteer toast is on a `puppeteer` channel that a GM can switch off in Blacksmith's settings. Stamping a template, and a Capture Area that adds members, play a soft sound on your screen.
- **Move Pivot.** Put a group's pin anywhere, not only at the centre: the prow of a ship, a wagon's hitch, a door's hinge. Turn on Move Pivot for the group and drag the pin to where the grip should be. The pin moves and every part, rider and the body stay where they are; Move Pivot then switches itself off, and the group moves and turns about the new grip. From a macro: `setMovePivot`.
- **Double-click a pin to configure its group.** Double-clicking a group's pin opens the Group Editor on that group, with its members showing. GM only.
- **Riders.** A group can carry tokens, such as a ship's crew. Tick Tokens board when they move onto it and any token that moves onto the group boards it (or is created on it, by dropping an actor from the sidebar), or select tokens that are on the group and choose Board Selected. Riders are carried when the group moves and swing round the pin when it turns. A rider can be walked anywhere inside the group and rides from where it is put; walked off the group, it dismounts where it stands and the GM is told. A token dismounted on purpose stays off until it has left the group and come back. Deleting the group, or a rider's token, lets it go. From a macro: `mount`, `dismount` and `setMountable`. Riders jump to their new place for now, as lights and walls do.
- **Library entries remember their actor.** Save a group that is a target and every stamp of it comes with its own body for the same actor, sized to that stamp (including a turned one). The Library lists which actor an entry represents. A stamp in a world without that actor has no body and tells the GM.
- **Make a group a target.** Drag an actor from the sidebar onto a group's body row in the Group Editor (or choose Use Selected Token) and the group gets a body: an invisible, locked token of that actor, as big as the group, that follows it. Click anywhere on the group to select or target the actor; attacks, damage and the sheet work as for any monster. The actor's picture never shows on the map, and the token is unlinked unless you tick Linked. The body is refitted when the group moves or turns and when parts are added or removed (lights and sounds do not count towards its size). Deleting the group deletes its body; deleting the body leaves the group. From a macro: `setBody`, `adoptBody`, `clearBody` and `setBodyLinked`.
- **Regions with a cutout.** The group bounds overlay draws each shape of a region as its own outline, so a region with a cutout is no longer joined by long straight lines. A region whose shapes were changed by hand (a cutout adds or reshapes shapes) is recorded again where it stands before it is carried, so old and new geometry are never mixed.
- **Smooth pin groups.** Dragging a pin, the tiles, drawings and notes of its group follow the pointer. When a pin moves or turns any other way (a drop, Shift + wheel, the editor's rotate buttons) every player sees the group ease to its new place over about a third of a second, turning along an arc, instead of jumping.
- **Turn a group with the mouse wheel.** A pin group now faces where its pin faces. Select the pin and hold Shift while scrolling the wheel (or scroll on a trackpad) over the board: the whole group turns about the pin, 5 degrees a notch. The editor's heading field and rotate buttons turn the same pin, so they always agree. Groups saved earlier keep their heading. Needs the Blacksmith version with pin rotation.
- **Click a member to select it.** The icon beside each member in the Group Editor selects that object on the canvas, switching to its layer; Shift adds it to the selection, and the view pans to it if it is off screen.
- **Member ids stay in view.** Each member in the editor shows its id beside its name, and clicking it copies the full UUID. Renaming a member never changes its id.
- **Tabs, and a tool window.** The Group Editor is now a Blacksmith tool window, so it follows the Light, Dark and Glass themes, can use the compact title bar, and keeps its place. It has three tabs: Groups, Library and Tools. Show group bounds is an icon in the title bar. Tools holds Update all groups and a Diagnose button for each group.
- **Deleting groups.** Deleting a group offers to delete its parts from the scene too, not only the group. Deleting a group also deletes its body token.
- **Regions in groups.** A region can be a member, so zones such as a campsite or a trap area move and turn with the rest. Every shape in the region is carried: rectangles (by their anchor point), ellipses, circles, cones, rings, lines, polygons and emanations. Regions with a token or grid-cell shape cannot be turned freely and are refused with a reason. Add them with Add Selected.
- **The grip is always a pin.** A group is moved and turned by its pin, with everything a pin offers (hide it, restyle it, limit who can move it). A group that followed a token in an earlier build is converted when the world loads: it gets a pin where the token was, nothing moves, and the token is left as an ordinary token. Make a group something that can be attacked with a body (an actor's invisible token), not by making the token the grip.
- **Tiles are placed correctly in v14.** A v14 tile's `x, y` is its texture anchor point, the centre by default, not its top-left corner, and it turns about that point. Puppeteer had treated it as top-left, so a tile was half its size out whenever a group turned or a template was stamped. Group records saved before this are repaired from where the tiles are when the world loads, and exported templates from before are refused with a clear message (template format version 2).
- **Failures are no longer silent.** An error while moving or turning a group, or while centring its pivot, is shown to the GM instead of only logged, and is handed back to whoever asked. Centre Pivot checks that the pin really moved and puts the group back if it did not. A member that another module stops from moving raises one notification.
- **Library and stamping.** Save a group to a library kept in a world setting, then stamp it onto any scene, as many times as wanted, at the centre of the view. Each stamp creates new tiles, lights, sounds, walls, a new pin and a new group, so two stamps are two independent copies. Templates can be imported from and exported to JSON files. In the Group Editor and the console API.
- **Group bounds overlay.** An optional overlay draws the box that holds each group (turned with the group), its pivot and the way it faces. Toggled from the toolbar, the Group Editor or the pin menu; remembered per user.
- **Member names.** Each member of a group can be given a name in the Group Editor; the name survives hand edits and travels in templates.
- **Diagnosis.** `api.diagnose(groupId)` reports where a group's pin, record and members are against where they should be.
- **Contributor checks.** `node tools/check-scripts.mjs` (scripts parse as ES modules, every import resolves), `node tools/check-blacksmith-only.mjs` (fails on core calls Blacksmith replaces, such as `Hooks.on`, `game.socket`, `ui.notifications`), and `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` (geometry and templates).
- **Group templates.** A group can be exported as a portable template (offsets and a snapshot of every member), saved as JSON from the Group Editor, and turned into creation data for any pivot and heading. This is the format prefabs will use.
- **Centre Pivot.** Moves a group's pin to the centre of what it holds without moving any member, so the group turns about its own middle. In the Group Editor and the pin menu.
- **Manual edits are kept.** Moving, turning or resizing a member by hand updates where it sits in the group, instead of being undone on the next move. Capturing waits for the group to settle first. The follower logs what it writes under Blacksmith debug and warns when a member does not take the position it wrote.
- **Capture Area.** Drag a box and every tile, drawing, light, sound, wall and note whose centre is inside joins the group, across all layers, using Blacksmith's area pick. Available from the Group Editor and the pin menu.
- **Group Editor, toolbar and pin menu.** A Group Editor window lists the groups on the scene with their members, heading, rename, add and remove selected, select members, go to pin and delete. The Blacksmith toolbar has Puppeteer Groups and New Group tools, and right-clicking a group's pin offers Add Selected, Remove Selected, Select Members, Rotate 90 either way and Open Group Editor. Deleting a member's document removes it from its groups.
- **Pin-anchored groups.** A group is a record in a scene flag, anchored on a Blacksmith pin. Dragging the pin, or changing the group's heading, moves and rotates every member (Tile, Drawing, AmbientLight, AmbientSound, Wall, Note) rigidly. The active GM applies it from `updateScene`, one queued write per group. Driven from a console API on the module for now.
- **Bootstrap.** Puppeteer waits for Blacksmith, refuses to start if its ready stage failed or any API member it uses is missing, registers its console label and registers itself with Blacksmith.
- **Module scaffold.** Folder layout, release and wiki workflows, and the design plan. No feature code yet.
