# Carrying Tokens as Riders

**Audience:** game masters whose group should carry tokens, such as a ship's crew or the passengers on a wagon.

Setting this up is GM only. Players board by moving their own tokens.

## Let tokens board

On the group's row in the Group Editor, turn on **Tokens board when they move onto it**. From then on:

- A token that moves onto the group boards it, whether the GM or a player moved it.
- A token created on the group, by dragging an actor from the sidebar onto it, boards it.

With the option off, nothing boards by itself. Select tokens that are on the group and choose **Board Selected** instead.

Every token that moves onto the group boards while the option is on, enemies included. Turn the option off, or dismount the token, if that is not what you want.

## What riders do

- **They are carried.** When the group moves or turns, its riders go with it, and a turn swings them round the pin. They move through walls and ignore speed while being carried.
- **They can walk about.** A rider can be moved anywhere on the group, and it rides from wherever it was put. Turn off **Riders move freely** under **Behaviour** to put a rider straight back instead.
- **They get off by leaving.** Move a rider off the group and it dismounts where it stands. You are told. Only the solid part of a picture counts as the group, so the transparent corners of a ship's image are water, not deck; turn off **Solid parts only** under **Behaviour** to count the whole box instead.

## Put a rider off on purpose

Choose **Dismount** beside the rider on the group's row. It stays where it is and does not climb back aboard while it is still on the group. It boards again only after it has left the group and come back. **Board Selected** boards it again at once.

## Announce boarding

Turn on **Announce boarding** under **Behaviour** and a public card, with a soft sound, tells the table when a token boards or leaves.

## When something is deleted

Deleting the group lets every rider go where it stands. Deleting a rider's token takes it off the group.
