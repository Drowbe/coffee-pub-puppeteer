# What Players See and Can Do

**Audience:** players at a table that uses Puppeteer.

You do not need to do anything to use a group. The GM builds and moves them. This is what you will notice.

## What you see

- **Groups move as one.** A cart, ship or drawbridge moves and turns as a whole when the GM moves its pin. Depending on how the GM set it, the group glides to its new place or jumps there. Lights, walls and sounds on it always jump.
- **You never see a group's editor.** The Group Editor and the pin tools are GM only.

## When your token is aboard

- **Boarding.** If the GM allows it, move your token onto the group and it boards. You can then walk about on the group.
- **Being carried.** When the group moves or turns, your token goes with it, through walls and without using your speed.
- **Getting off.** Move your token off the group and it dismounts where it stands.
- **Chat.** If the GM turned on announcements, a card appears in chat when a token boards or leaves.

## Attacking a group

If the GM made the group something that can be attacked, click anywhere over it to select or target it, and attack it as you would any monster. You cannot drag it. Its picture is not shown on the map, so you only see its outline when you point at it.

## What you cannot do

You cannot move, turn or edit a group yourself, unless the GM has given you permission on the group's pin, in which case dragging the pin moves the whole group without walls or a movement ruler.
