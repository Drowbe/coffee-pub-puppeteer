# Making a Group Something That Can Be Attacked

**Audience:** game masters whose group should have hit points, such as a siege engine, a wagon or a monster built from scenery.

Everything here is GM only. Most groups do not need this: a group moves and turns without any actor. Give a group a body only when players should be able to target and attack it.

## Give a group a body

1. Open the Group Editor and find the group on the **Scene Groups** tab.
2. Drag an actor from the Actors sidebar onto the group's body row. Or select a token already on the map and choose **Use Selected Token**.
3. An invisible token of that actor appears, as big as the group. Click anywhere over the group to select or target it. Attacks, damage and the actor's sheet work as for any monster.

The actor's picture is never shown on the map. You never place, size or move the token: it follows the group and changes size when you add or remove parts or turn the group.

Actors in a compendium must be imported into the world before you can drop them.

## Choose whether it shares hit points

The body is unlinked by default, so two copies of a group have separate hit points. Turn on **Linked** under **Body** to share one set of hit points with the actor.

## Things to know

- You cannot drag the body. Move the group by its pin.
- Lights, sounds and notes do not count towards the body's size.
- At a diagonal heading the body is a little larger than the group, because a token's outline does not turn.
- Use the cross on the row to remove the body. The group, its pin and its parts stay. Deleting the group deletes its body.
- Dropping an actor onto the map over a group does not make a body. It boards the actor as a rider if the group allows it (see [Carrying tokens as riders](userguide-riders.md)).
