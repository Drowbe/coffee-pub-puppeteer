# The Library

**Audience:** game masters who want more than one copy of a group.

Everything here is GM only. The library is where you keep saved copies of groups so you can place them again, on any scene, as many times as you like. It is shared by every scene in the world.

## Save a group to the library

On the **Scene Groups** tab, choose the Library icon (the boxes, as on the Library tab) in the group's bar. A copy of the group is stored under its name. Later changes to the group on the scene do not change the saved copy, and the saved copy does not change the group.

## Place a copy

1. Open the **Library** tab.
2. Choose **Fabricate** on the entry. The group follows your pointer with its pin under the crosshair, shown at its true size; click the map where the pin goes (right-click or Escape cancels). It becomes a new group with its own pin, its own tiles, lights, walls and sounds, and the names you gave the parts.

Each copy is independent. Two copies are two groups: move, turn or delete one and the other is untouched.

If the saved group could be attacked (see [Making a group something that can be attacked](userguide-targets.md)), each copy comes with its own token for the same actor, with its own hit points, and the entry shows which actor it represents. If that actor is not in your world, the copy has no token and you are told.

## Sort the library into categories

Choose **New Category** at the bottom of the **Library** tab and name it, such as Vehicles or Buildings. Each category is a card that opens and closes and holds its entries. Drag an entry onto a category to file it there, and onto **Uncategorised** to take it out again. Click a category's name to rename it. The trash button on a category deletes it; its entries are kept and go to Uncategorised. Two categories cannot have the same name, however it is written.

A category travels with an entry that is exported: the file names it, and importing the entry files it under the category of that name, making one if there is none.

## Name and describe an entry

Each entry shows a picture of the group, its name and a description. The picture is drawn when you save the entry, or update it, from the group's own parts: the tiles' images where they sit, with the walls, lights, sounds, drawings and regions as simple marks. It shows the group alone, with no map under it. If it cannot be drawn, the entry has a placeholder instead. Choose the pencil beside either to change it, then press Enter or click away to keep it, or Escape to put it back.

## Change an entry

On the entry, choose **Replace from Group**. A list of the groups on this scene appears; choose the one the entry should become. The entry keeps its name and description and takes the parts, token and picture of that group. Copies you have already fabricated are not changed. Choose Cancel to back out; the button is greyed out when the scene has no groups.

## Share an entry

Choose the export button on an entry to save it as a file, and **Import** on the **Library** tab to add an entry from a file. Importing adds a new entry; it never replaces one.

## Remove an entry

Choose the trash button on an entry and confirm. Groups already placed on scenes are not affected, and deleting a group on a scene does not remove its entry.
