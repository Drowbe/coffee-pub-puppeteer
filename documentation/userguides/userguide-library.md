# The Library

**Audience:** game masters who want more than one copy of a group.

Everything here is GM only. The library is where you keep saved copies of groups so you can place them again, on any scene, as many times as you like. It is shared by every scene in the world.

## Save a group to the library

On the **Scene Groups** tab, choose **Save to Library** on the group. A copy of the group is stored under its name. Later changes to the group on the scene do not change the saved copy, and the saved copy does not change the group.

## Place a copy

1. Open the **Library** tab.
2. Choose **Stamp** beside the entry. A new group appears at the centre of your view, with its own pin, its own tiles, lights, walls and sounds, and the names you gave the parts.

Each stamp is independent. Two stamps are two groups: move, turn or delete one and the other is untouched.

If the saved group could be attacked (see [Making a group something that can be attacked](userguide-targets.md)), each stamp comes with its own body for the same actor, with its own hit points, and the entry shows which actor it represents. If that actor is not in your world, the stamp has no body and you are told.

## Share an entry

Choose the export button beside an entry to save it as a file, and **Import** on the **Library** tab to add an entry from a file. Importing adds a new entry; it never replaces one.

## Remove an entry

Choose the trash button beside an entry and confirm. Groups already placed on scenes are not affected, and deleting a group on a scene does not remove its entry.
